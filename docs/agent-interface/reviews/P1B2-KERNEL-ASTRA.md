## Why the private kernel passes this gate

Its authority is five named step documents, fixed creation descriptors and derived filenames, canonical before/after records, and shared semantic validators. It is not an arbitrary patch executor. `_validate_core_output` checks canonical jobs/contexts and the entire prescribed unsuccessful delta; `_validate_evidence` grounds actions, monitor/faults, prior epoch, proof and transitive outputs. `_validate_archives` queries the actual private SQLite snapshot against the complete canonical source and actual plan. `_validate_b2_database` verifies ordered steps, retained histories and the complete resolution sets. Matching digest strings alone do not satisfy these checks.

A full history can permanently prevent another operation; no eviction or infinite-throughput claim follows. Refactoring merely because the module grew would add risk without closing an identified defect. Retain these common validators and small fixed stages; do not introduce a new recovery framework.
